    How to read this

    decoded ✓ packet decoded and its CRC/checksum verified - this exact device.

    signal structure a clearly man-made signal no decoder knows yet; identified by band.

    RF energy a transmission we can measure but not demodulate (P25, TETRA, LTE, LoRa); identified by band and behaviour.

    Distance is estimated from signal strength and each emitter's typical power. Treat it as "next door / down the street / across town", not a position. Walls, antennas and terrain move it by a factor of 2-3.
